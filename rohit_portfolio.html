The Story
ENGINEER.
ANALYST.
FOUNDER.

I started with Electrical Engineering at MIT Pune — circuit design, signal systems, and the physics beneath every machine. That rigour pushed me into industry: running structured data analysis across 500+ alternator units weekly at Auto Ignition Limited, cutting defect rates and building validation systems from the ground up.

Then I asked a harder question: what can software do that hardware cannot? That led me to Python, machine learning, and causal inference — and eventually to Mindmic, a native macOS AI dictation app I designed, built, and shipped entirely in Swift and SwiftUI. Real users. Real production. Zero wrappers.

I move across the full stack — from circuit boards to Swift code to causal inference pipelines. International exposure at ICTP, Italy. Three IIT certifications. Always building the next thing. Based in Pune, Maharashtra.
